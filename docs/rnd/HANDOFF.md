Each attempt is capped at 600 seconds from acquisition, each subprocess is bounded by the remaining budget, and ownership is checked again before publication. Its smoke gate runs with networking disabled and verifies authenticated capability access and persistence across restart. Ordinary host port publishing cannot expose its loopback listener. This validates container plumbing only.
- Native research capture now makes queue admission and the final marker atomic; queued optional work is skipped after disable. Work already executing may finish. Compiler/JVM/unit evidence remains distinct from physical recording continuity, performance, thermal, storage, accessibility and device acceptance.

## Separate-process federated integration

[Run 38191469774](https://github.com/thepackproof/packproof-v2/actions/runs/38191469774) passed at source `24ba5224fe5d7830e2ebebcc9c975b07e2db2235`. The complete Linux federated suite passed **54 tests**, with no skipped tests and two warnings. Source-isolation checks passed; unrelated jobs were explicitly skipped. The earlier failed attempts remain in the current validation record: the first found that Flower consumes stage metadata, and the next exposed cleanup deadlock after killing a process waiting on a shared event. The endpoint preserves the stage before handing the message to Flower, and process cleanup now uses a one-writer stop byte without a waiter lock, followed by bounded joins and termination. The successful run includes real separate-process failure and cleanup cases.

The [transport implementation](../../research/federated/TRANSPORT.md#profile-bound-endpointcoordinator-and-separate-process-lab) provides reusable profile-bound partner and coordinator APIs. The laboratory wrapper starts 20-64 separate endpoint processes over authenticated TLS; tests exercise 20-process rounds. Signed model, recipe, enrolled roster, run and exact dataset bindings are verified before training. Fresh consent is checked by the endpoint before and after stages and at a candidate-bound checkpoint after unmasking. Persistent admission IDs prevent replay across restart. The coordinator reserves cumulative privacy spend before aggregation, and incomplete rounds, unavailable peers, withdrawal or budget rejection cannot store a new candidate. Earlier candidates remain unchanged. No model is automatically promoted.

These checks close the identified implementation gaps in F10-02, F10-03 and F10-06 and extend the consent/accounting evidence in F10-01 and F10-04. They establish one controlled laboratory implementation, with zero independently governed partner organizations. The separate processes are not an OS sandbox. The coordinator still sees the pre-noise aggregate, and checkpoint acknowledgments do not create an atomic distributed withdrawal transaction: withdrawal after the final acknowledgment may race candidate storage. Independent governance, a reviewed commit/withdrawal boundary, security/privacy review and representative utility remain required. Synthetic quality outcomes are retained without promoting a failing candidate or treating a protocol pass as useful-model evidence.
