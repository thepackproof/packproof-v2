## Prepared tools and protocols

- [Physical study packet](physical-study-packet.md) and [intake formats/commands](physical-intake-format.md): protocol freezing, exact media hashes, lineage, denominators, calibration and passive comparisons.
- [Reviewer protocol](reviewer-protocol.md) and [offline toolkit](reviewer-toolkit.md): private coordinator manifest, separate reviewer packets and scoring. A provisional feasibility design uses 24 independent cases and 12 reviewers per study, two independent reference annotators and a third person to review disagreements. The same 12 reviewers can do both studies with distinct case clusters and counterbalanced study order. These numbers are planning proposals, not statistically justified qualification thresholds.
- [Proof performance and cryptographic review](privacy-performance-review.md): supported tiny-image measurements, exact trust boundary and review questions. Six successful proofs establish those measured local cases only; no production-size or video throughput is claimed.
- [Independent witness packet](witness-operator-qualification.md): structural validation does not authenticate organizations, keys, agreements or service availability. Current test witnesses do not count as independent operators.
- [Distributed privacy analysis](federated-privacy-review.md): ideal variance is a necessary calculation, not a complete privacy proof. In the illustrative 24-enrolled/20-surviving/4-colluding scenario, calibrating noise to all 24 originals produces effective multiplier 3.266 instead of target 4. The installed encoder clips values before aggregation, so a reviewed quantization/noise mechanism is still required. Distributed mode remains disabled.

## Validation scope and decisions

The new tools have targeted offline checks on Windows and Linux. Actual local proof generation is measured separately from receipt-parser tests. Synthetic reviewer packets are software demonstrations; they do not count as a human study. A packet passing structural validation does not establish consent, physical independence, metrological traceability or a security/privacy claim.

The source and final validation receipt are linked from the status ledger. No backend, mobile, desktop, model, proof circuit, native capture driver or dependency version changes are part of this preparation. Existing runtime source-specific evidence remains in [the earlier follow-up receipt](../validation/2026-10-11-follow-up.json). Only affected preparation checks are rerun.

No outside person has been contacted or enrolled. To advance beyond the small phone pilot, the owner needs to supply access to materials/participants and nominate the relevant independent specialists/operators or provide a budget and contact route. Once those dependencies exist, remaining engineering and measurements can target actual hardware, mechanisms and service contracts. None of these documents authorizes a production deployment.
