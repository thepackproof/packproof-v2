# Existing-data reconstruction: explicit partitions and versioned projections

Status: internal candidate; production gates remain open.

The operational case is a working view of source assertions. It may exist before a shipment Proof, and it does not change the immutable Proof identity or historical signed manifests.

## Identity and association

Version-one batches retain their exact contract. A separately approved version-two source policy binds composite key components and revision meaning to the complete reviewed source configuration. Composite keys preserve exact values and leading zeros; quantity, file hashes and timestamp proximity never become business identity. Source-as-of revisions use actual reported boundaries, preserve late snapshots and identify the applicable active revision independently of arrival order.

A multi-order observation requires an approved explicit allocation policy. Immutable per-case projection decisions select its permitted assertions and relationships; the compatibility primary-case pointer cannot grant access to another partition. Reused shared IDs in unrelated receipts do not join cases. Human entity aliases and namespace rules bind entity type, parent scope, cardinality and effective interval. A successor decision preserves the previous decision and invalidates current derived views.

All case search, counts, extraction, issue history, exports and retention use the same current membership predicates. Unauthorized neighbors produce a restricted indicator without identifiers or counts.

## Reads and comparisons

Ordinary case detail is SQL-paged. A bounded worker rebuilds complete active comparisons into versioned derived tables; receipt and assertion ledgers remain authoritative. Case revision, source policy signature and time expiry bind the materialized result. Pending or stale evaluation produces unavailable comparison totals rather than plausible partial totals. Source accounting counters reconcile once from the durable ledger and then update transactionally.

Qualified operational absence rules bind exact current source revisions and declared vocabulary. A label event needs an actual event time and matching carrier account/service. Expected handling-unit receipt needs an explicit planned deadline and shipment/stop relationship. Unknown or stale prerequisites suppress a business absence finding.

## Preservation and sharing

Case exports contain authorized field extracts and omission manifests, never complete shared originals. A bounded per-export original cache avoids repeatedly retrieving the same exact version. The additive signed v2 package inventories the frozen v1 content and reuses the existing signing and independently pinned trust mechanisms. Its source/profile, organization, environment, key and expiry bindings are deployment inputs.

Source disposition uses a separate worker-only exact-version deletion capability. Independent approvals bind the entire original's impact, policy and inherited holds. Destructive execution rechecks current authority and retention before issuing an exact-version operation. Completed collector compaction commands retain receipt, grant, configuration, digest and current scope checks. Immutable normalized audit history and backups are not silently erased.

## Compatibility and qualification

Migrations 110–113 and old release policies remain frozen. Migrations 114–116 and the explicit reconstruction-v2/116 release profile are additive. New flags default off. Rollback pauses new admission/readers and preserves queues, keys, originals, ledgers and generated packages; compatible roll-forward repair replaces destructive reset.

Automated fixtures establish implementation behavior. Actual Windows 11 installed-flow/usability acceptance, authorized real feed contracts, production IAM/storage/signing/restore review and the pilot investigation study remain external qualification requirements.
