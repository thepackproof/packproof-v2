**Promise:** Assemble existing approved records into a coherent, source-linked fulfillment history and identify missing or conflicting documentation.

**Boundaries:** No ERP/WMS/TMS replacement, upstream write-back, mandatory capture task, automated credit decision, fault attribution or claim-outcome promise.

**Approvals to record before actual imports:** Local/site owner; authorized IT and data controller; permitted fields and original attachments; secure ingress; verified key dictionary and source revision semantics; retention/deletion/hold process; operational storage/role/security preflight.

**Evaluation:** Use comparable operational questions and original materials. Stratify by complexity. Counterbalance order or use matched cases to reduce learning effects. Have the site SME adjudicate links and missing records. Preserve incomplete tasks and sample sizes in the report. Report observed distributions rather than promised ROI.

**Go/no-go:** No cross-site leakage or confidently incorrect joins in adjudicated examples; transparent omissions; useful retrieval/review experience; agreed measurable benefit at acceptable setup and reviewer burden. Only then consider approved recurring exports or one evidence-gap intervention.
