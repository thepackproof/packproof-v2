### Description

```text
Document the shipment while you pack it.

PackProof connects order details, packing video, and available shipping activity in a structured record called a Proof. Keep the evidence with the order it belongs to, ready to review and share when questions arise.

RECORD THE PACKING PROCESS
Show the item, packaging, and final seal in one continuous video. Review your recording and confirm what you are shipping before submitting it. iPhone recordings are silent.

KEEP THE RECORD TOGETHER
Open saved recordings alongside order context and a timeline of recorded activity. Check available carrier reports from the same Proof. Shipment tracking depends on the carrier and connection available for that shipment.

FIND THE PROOF YOU NEED
Search your Proofs and use Needs attention, All, and Completed to find the relevant record. Proof completion and delivery status are shown separately.

SHARE THE EVIDENCE
Create a viewing link to the shared record and saved recordings. Review the included information before sharing, choose a link expiry, and revoke future access when needed. Revocation cannot recall copies someone has already saved.

BRING IN ORDER CONTEXT
Enter shipment details directly. From apps that provide compatible content, share plain text or a web link to PackProof, then review and finish preparing the order in the app. Connected-store options depend on provider and account availability.

PackProof is neutral evidence infrastructure for e-commerce. It records what was captured, submitted, and reported. It does not independently authenticate an item, establish events outside the record, or decide a refund, claim, or dispute.

A PackProof account and an internet connection are required to submit and access server records.
```
