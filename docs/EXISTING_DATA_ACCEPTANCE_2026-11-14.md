- CSV snapshot-boundary corpus and integration suites: actual exporter marker, source time and reported row control total; exact original verification, forged/mixed/unknown controls, historical ordering and future-clock suppression. A recent file or PackProof-derived count never supplies missing source controls.
- `backend/tests/reconstruction-pages.test.ts` and `identity-projection.test.ts`: bounded SQL pages, complete versioned comparisons, revision cursors, current identity decisions and restricted neighbors.
- `backend/tests/operational-presence-rules.test.ts`: exact qualified policy bindings and suppression for absent, stale, copied or unknown prerequisites.
- `backend/tests/source-retention.test.ts`, `reconstruction-signing.test.ts`, `source-disposition-s3.test.ts`: immutable decisions, scope/time/hold changes, frozen signing inventory and exact owner/version-bound SDK operations; synthetic transports perform no live cloud deletion.
- `backend/tests/reconstruction-runtime-privileges.test.ts` and `reconstruction-worker-roles.test.ts`: frozen113 and explicit116 role boundaries, actual restricted SQL-role command flow, real PostgreSQL CI gate.
- Windows service/shared corpus/compaction suites and desktop checks: stable legacy/v2 interpretation and refusal to discard mismatched or pending work.
- Historical job and collector lane suites: exact manifest approval, current source/credential/revision checks, receipt-bound overlap, later live snapshot preservation, independent budgets and restart without changing retained submission identities. Historical observations do not qualify uncollected stages or exporter completeness.
- Portal component tests and production build: visible pending/restricted/unavailable states, explicit source policy and mapping approvals, accurate signed-export availability.

CI links, exact source and installer digest are recorded in PR164. Benchmark reports identify engine/hardware, fixture mix, seeded-history exclusions, queue lag and measured percentiles; a synthetic CI result does not qualify a customer's production system.

## External acceptance collection

For Windows11, record exact installer/API/browser/source versions; each durable interruption boundary; retained queue, key and receipt reconciliation; first and unattended second accepted source file; and administrator versus ordinary worker actions. Record failed paths as well as successful ones.

For each of the three pilot feeds, record owner approval and exact source/exporter version; row grain; identifier namespace/reuse/cardinality; correction and snapshot/delta behavior; units/status meaning; time offsets/quality; copied lineage; publication convention; completeness control and retention scope. Missing information narrows the advertised capability.

For the20-investigation study, use comparable labeled case mix. Record manual and PackProof elapsed time, median andp90, evidence completeness, association accuracy/unresolved rate, incorrect conclusions, source outage understanding and mandatory worker actions. The50% median improvement is a test hypothesis, not a promised outcome.
